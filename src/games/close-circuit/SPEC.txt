UPDATE (Oct 2026): rule changes for NORMAL mode only (advanced mode is unchanged)

1. A valid closed circuit must contain every single card on the board. Several separate
   loops, or a loop plus stray cards, do not win.
2. Cards no longer have to be placed next to existing cards (the very first card of the game
   still goes in the center). But all the cards you place in ONE turn must be adjacent to
   each other.
3. The cards placed in one turn must form a straight line of 1 to 3 cards (one row or one
   column). Any mix of straights and turns is fine (straight, turn, straight is OK), but an
   L shape (two cards in a row plus a third beside them) is not allowed.
4. A challenged player (both modes) may use ALL the cards left in the deck. In normal mode
   they are also free of the straight-line rule: any number of cards, any shape, anywhere.
