UPDATE (Oct 2026): rule changes for NORMAL mode only (advanced mode is unchanged)

1. A valid closed circuit must contain every single card on the board. Several separate
   loops, or a loop plus stray cards, do not win.
2. Cards no longer have to be placed next to existing cards (the very first card of the game
   still goes in the center). But all the cards you place in ONE turn must be adjacent to
   each other.
3. The cards placed in one turn must form a straight line of 1 to 3 cards (one row or one
   column). Any mix of straights and turns is fine (straight, turn, straight is OK), but an
   L shape (two cards in a row plus a third beside them) is not allowed.
4. A challenged player (both modes) may use ALL the cards left in the deck. In normal mode
   they are also free of the straight-line rule: any number of cards, any shape, anywhere.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
UPDATE (Oct 2026): optional coordinates on the board (both modes)

The setup screen has a Coordinates setting (on by default). When on, x and y numbers sit on
the sides of the board. The center cell is (0, 0); x increases to the right, y increases up.
Turn it off to hide the numbers. Gameplay is the same either way.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
UPDATE (Oct 2026): tap a tile you just placed to edit it (this turn's draft only)

Normal (and advanced challenge/solve from the face-up deck): tap the middle of the tile to
switch between straight and turn; tap the right side to rotate. A straight has 2 directions
(click twice to return); a turn has 4 (click four times to return).

Advanced hand play: the card type is fixed by the hand, so only the right side rotates.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
UPDATE (Oct 2026): measure distance on the board

Tap the Measure button, tap a start cell, then tap the other cell. Taps do not place cards
while Measure is on; tap Measure again to stop. On a computer you can also right-click a
cell, then scroll the wheel (or a trackpad left/right) to change how far the other end is
along x. Shift+scroll moves along y. Moving the pointer to another cell also works. The
label is the number of cells apart in display coordinates (center = 0, 0). Right-click the
start again or press Esc to stop. Rotate with R / the Rotate button (right-click is no
longer rotate). Long-press on a phone is ignored so it does not steal a placement tap.
